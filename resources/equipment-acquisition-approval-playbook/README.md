# Equipment Acquisition and Approval Playbook

A nine-step purchase control process with a worked example: request form, business justification, specification worksheet, approval tracker, authorization checklist, receiving and inspection checklist, registration handover checklist.

**Use it for:** Controlling equipment purchases before the money is spent.
