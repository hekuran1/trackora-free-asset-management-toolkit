# Online calculators and planners

Five free tools that run in your browser on trackora.cloud. No registration; what you type stays in your browser. Results are estimates from your own inputs, not guaranteed savings.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Equipment Waste and Savings Calculator](https://trackora.cloud/equipment-waste-savings-calculator?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=resource-center&utm_content=equipment-waste-savings-calculator) | Estimate capital tied up in unused equipment and the yearly cost of loss, with savings scenarios. |
| [Equipment Replacement and Refresh Planner](https://trackora.cloud/equipment-replacement-refresh-planner?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=resource-center&utm_content=equipment-replacement-refresh-planner) | Turn age, condition and criticality into review priorities and a five-year replacement forecast. |
| [Equipment Lifecycle Cost Calculator](https://trackora.cloud/equipment-lifecycle-cost-calculator?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=resource-center&utm_content=equipment-lifecycle-cost-calculator) | Compare the full cost of owning two equipment options, per year and per month. |
| [Manual IT Operations Cost Calculator](https://trackora.cloud/manual-it-operations-cost-calculator?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=resource-center&utm_content=manual-it-operations-cost-calculator) | Count the hours and labor cost of manual equipment administration and what a reduction would be worth. |
| [Asset Tracking Software ROI Calculator](https://trackora.cloud/asset-tracking-software-roi-calculator?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=resource-center&utm_content=asset-tracking-software-roi-calculator) | Build a business case from your own assumptions, with conservative, moderate and optimistic scenarios. |

All fourteen free resources: https://trackora.cloud/resources?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=resource-center&utm_content=calculators
