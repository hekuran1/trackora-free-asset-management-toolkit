# Trackora Free Resource Library

Five professionally designed business resources for organizations that buy, budget for, issue and audit equipment. All free. No sign-up, no email, no account.

| Resource | Format | What it does | Download all |
|---|---|---|---|
| [IT Equipment Budget Planner](it-equipment-budget-planner/README.md) | Excel | Plan purchasing budgets, department allocations and forecasts. | [ZIP](it-equipment-budget-planner/it-equipment-budget-planner-complete.zip) |
| [Equipment Replacement Cost Calculator](equipment-replacement-calculator/README.md) | Excel | Estimate replacement costs and annual refresh budgets. | [ZIP](equipment-replacement-calculator/equipment-replacement-calculator-complete.zip) |
| [IT Procurement Comparison Toolkit](it-procurement-comparison/README.md) | Excel | Compare quotations on total cost and weighted criteria. | [ZIP](it-procurement-comparison/it-procurement-comparison-complete.zip) |
| [Employee Equipment Policy Pack](employee-equipment-policy-pack/README.md) | Word + PDF | Seven editable policy and form documents. | [ZIP](employee-equipment-policy-pack/employee-equipment-policy-pack-complete.zip) |
| [IT Asset Audit Preparation Pack](it-asset-audit-preparation-pack/README.md) | Word, PDF + Excel | Evidence log, findings tracker, timeline and guides. | [ZIP](it-asset-audit-preparation-pack/it-asset-audit-preparation-pack-complete.zip) |

Each folder has its own README with previews, instructions and individual file links.

## Choosing a resource

- **Planning what to buy and when:** Budget Planner, then the Replacement Cost Calculator.
- **Choosing a supplier:** Procurement Comparison Toolkit.
- **Setting rules for staff:** Employee Equipment Policy Pack.
- **Getting ready for an audit:** IT Asset Audit Preparation Pack.
- **Starting an equipment register:** the original [starter templates](../README.md) in this repository.
